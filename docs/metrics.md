# Metric catalog

Generated by `make parse` from `models/semantic.yml`. See [modeling decisions](semantics.md) for time, coverage and null policies.

## Reported net assets (`reported_net_assets`)

Sum of reported NET_ASSETS at the latest report date in the selected period. No carry-forward and no summing snapshots over time. Nulls are unreported; this extract is not corporate AUM.

- Grain: fund × report date; latest date within each requested time bucket
- Units: USD
- Owner: Distribution Analytics (lab role)
- Synonyms: net assets, reported fund assets
- Certification: educational / not externally certified

## Fund count (`fund_count`)

Distinct fund IDs at the latest report date in the selected period, including funds with missing net assets. Same-date population as reported net assets.

- Grain: fund × report date; latest date within each requested time bucket
- Units: funds
- Owner: Distribution Analytics (lab role)
- Synonyms: number of funds, reporting funds
- Certification: educational / not externally certified

## Gross sales (`gross_sales`)

Sum of reported sales of fund shares, including exchanges and excluding dividend/distribution reinvestments. Omnibus netting and acquisitions can be included by filers.

- Grain: fund × calendar month; additive across selected months
- Units: USD
- Owner: Distribution Analytics (lab role)
- Synonyms: subscriptions, sales
- Certification: educational / not externally certified

## Redemptions (`redemptions`)

Sum of reported shares redeemed or repurchased, including exchanges. Retain reported signs; negative amounts are flagged, never absolutized.

- Grain: fund × calendar month; additive across selected months
- Units: USD
- Owner: Distribution Analytics (lab role)
- Synonyms: withdrawals, repurchases
- Certification: educational / not externally certified

## Reinvestments (`reinvestments`)

Sum of shares sold in connection with reinvestments of dividends and distributions, as reported separately from sales.

- Grain: fund × calendar month; additive across selected months
- Units: USD
- Owner: Distribution Analytics (lab role)
- Synonyms: dividend reinvestments, distribution reinvestments
- Certification: educational / not externally certified

## Net flows excluding reinvestments (`net_flows_excluding_reinvestments`)

Gross sales total minus redemptions total. Each total uses its non-null observations; partial coverage can differ. If either entire total is missing, result is null. Reinvestments are excluded.

- Grain: fund × calendar month; additive across selected months
- Units: USD
- Owner: Distribution Analytics (lab role)
- Synonyms: net sales, external net flows
- Certification: educational / not externally certified

## Redemptions-to-sales ratio (`redemptions_to_sales_ratio`)

Total redemptions divided by total gross sales, never an average of fund ratios. Zero or missing denominator produces null. Partial numerator and denominator coverage can differ.

- Grain: fund × calendar month; additive across selected months
- Units: ratio
- Owner: Distribution Analytics (lab role)
- Synonyms: redemption pressure, outflow to inflow ratio
- Certification: educational / not externally certified
